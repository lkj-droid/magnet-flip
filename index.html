Magnet Flip: a five-tile twist on the match puzzle

Magnet Flip is a free puzzle game that plays in your browser in seconds. Every tap flips a small cross of magnetic tiles — the one you touch plus the four around it. Line up five of the same colour, watch the row snap away, and use the moves it pays back to keep the run going.

There is nothing to install and no account to make. The game is one small page that runs entirely on your device, so it works on a laptop, a tablet or a phone, online or offline once loaded.

Daily Challenge

One board a day, built from the UTC date, so every player in the world opens exactly the same puzzle. It resets at 00:00 UTC. Finish a run and copy a result with your score and an emoji map of your board to share anywhere.

Classic Run

A fresh random board every time. You start with 20 moves and there is no clock — clear lines to earn more and see how far one run can go. Your personal best is kept on your device.

How to play

  1. Tap any tile. It flips, and so do the tiles directly above, below, left and right of it — five in total, fewer at the edges of the board.
  2. Complete a line. When a whole row or column shows one colour, the line flashes and snaps away, and the tiles above drop down to fill the gap with fresh tiles from the top.
  3. Chain the drop. If the new tiles complete another line straight away, the clear chains. Each step of a chain scores higher and pays more moves.
  4. Spend your moves carefully. Every tap costs one move. Run out and the run ends — but a clear on your last move adds moves before the game checks.

How to play

The tap

Tap or click a tile and it flips, together with the tiles above, below, left and right of it. A tile in the middle of the board flips five; a corner flips three.

The clear

When a complete row or column shows a single colour, the line snaps away, the tiles above fall, and new tiles drop in from the top. If those new tiles complete another line, the clear chains and scores more.

Moves

Each tap costs one move. A cleared line pays +2 moves, and any clear on a chain step pays +3 more. You can hold at most 30. When your moves reach zero and nothing is left to clear, the run ends.

Controls

Arrow keys move the board cursor, Enter flips the selected tile, R restarts, M mutes, Esc closes dialogs.

Daily Challenge

Everyone gets the same board for the UTC day, and it resets at 00:00 UTC. When your run ends, press Share result to copy your score and an emoji map of your board.

Privacy

Magnet Flip has no sign-up, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts. The page makes no requests to any other server while you play.

Your personal best, today's Daily Challenge result and your sound setting are kept in your browser's local storage so the game can show them again next time. They stay on this device and are never uploaded. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.
